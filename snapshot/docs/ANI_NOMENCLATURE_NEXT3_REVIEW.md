## `ANI_REVIEW_054`: plantarum and argentoratensis

Current LPSN names are *Lactiplantibacillus plantarum* and *Lactiplantibacillus argentoratensis*. The latter was elevated from *Lactobacillus plantarum* subsp. *argentoratensis* and later transferred to *Lactiplantibacillus*. Those older argentoratensis combinations are homotypic synonyms at the same or a different rank; this history does not make the current two species synonyms. Sources: [argentoratensis LPSN](https://lpsn.dsmz.de/species/lactiplantibacillus-argentoratensis), [plantarum LPSN](https://lpsn.dsmz.de/species/lactiplantibacillus-plantarum), [Li et al. 2020, PMID 32250238](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32250238/), [Liu and Gu 2020, PMID 33112225](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33112225/).

The independent type-culture identifiers are DSM 16365 = CIP 108320 = CCUG 50787 = JCM 16169 = DKO 22 for argentoratensis, and DSM 20174 = ATCC 14917 = CIP 103151 = JCM 1149 for plantarum. DSMZ and JCM corroborate the argentoratensis culture relationship. Sources: [DSM 16365](https://www.dsmz.de/collection/catalogue/details/culture/DSM-16365), [JCM 16169](https://www.jcm.riken.jp/cgi-bin/jcm/jcm_number?JCM=16169), [DSM 20174](https://www.dsmz.de/collection/catalogue/details/culture/DSM-20174).

GCF_003641165.1 (DSM 16365; SAMN10174878) therefore has a source-declared identifier matching the independently defined argentoratensis type culture. Its BioSample wording names the older subspecies, which is explained by the homotypic nomenclatural history. GCF_014131735.1 (DSM 20174; SAMN11357169) similarly matches the plantarum type identifier despite older *Lactobacillus* wording. These agreements are identifier/scope checks, not independent authentication of the sequenced biological material. GCF_002220175.1 (strain dm; SAMN06855883) has no explicit type claim or independently checked type alias.

Existing, unchanged skani evidence contains two cross-label pairs, ANI 95.59 and 95.83 with reciprocal aligned fractions around 77–80%. The current nomenclature supports separate accepted names. No biological species-boundary decision, synonymization, or relabeling follows from these source-only checks; the cross-label genome relations remain unresolved.

## `ANI_REVIEW_059`: mundtii and the unvalidated spodopteracolus name

*Enterococcus mundtii* is validly published. Its type culture includes DSM 4838 = ATCC 43186 = MUTK 559 = NCDO 2375. GCF_002813755.1 (DSM 4838; SAMN06020033) matches that independent type-culture identifier. GCF_013394305.1 (15-1A; SAMD00177104) has no explicit type claim and no independently checked type alias. Sources: [mundtii LPSN](https://lpsn.dsmz.de/species/enterococcus-mundtii), [DSM 4838](https://www.dsmz.de/collection/catalogue/details/culture/DSM-4838).

**Material qualification:** LPSN marks “*Enterococcus spodopteracolus*” as not validly published and as a preferred name rather than a correct name. It lists IIL-Cl25 in its nomenclatural-type field while also recording a missing discernible protologue. NCBI calls the name effectively published but not validly published and lists the personal strain IIL-Cl25. The original comparative-genomics paper proposes this species and places it near mundtii; that proposal is distinct from valid publication under the ICNP. Sources: [spodopteracolus LPSN](https://lpsn.dsmz.de/species/enterococcus-spodopteracolus), [NCBI taxon 3034501](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/Taxonomy/Browser/wwwtax.cgi?id=3034501&mode=Info), [Gomes et al. 2023, PMID 37433981](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37433981/).

GCF_030441955.1 (IIL-Cl25; SAMN33735643) has the cached `TYPE_MATERIAL` flag and a matching personal identifier in both external databases. The permitted conclusion is a proposed/source type-reference identifier agreement for an unvalidated name. It must not be promoted to an authenticated type genome of a validly published species. No verified repository deposit alias was established in this review. This is not evidence that a repository deposit does not exist.

Existing cross-label ANI is 95.34 against the mundtii DSM 4838 genome and 96.46 against mundtii 15-1A, with unequal reciprocal aligned fractions. ## `ANI_REVIEW_064`: mictus and tenax

Both *Aerococcus mictus* and *Aerococcus tenax* are validly published current species resulting from the 2023 subdivision of the *A. urinae* complex. Their types differ: mictus UMB3440 = DSM 115699 = ATCC TSD-301 = CCUG 76532 = NR-58629; tenax UMB3669 = DSM 115700 = ATCC TSD-302 = CCUG 76531 = NR-58630. Sources: [mictus LPSN](https://lpsn.dsmz.de/species/aerococcus-mictus), [tenax LPSN](https://lpsn.dsmz.de/species/aerococcus-tenax), [DSM 115699](https://www.dsmz.de/collection/catalogue/details/culture/DSM-115699), [DSM 115700](https://www.dsmz.de/collection/catalogue/details/culture/DSM-115700), [Choi et al. 2023, PMID 37755156](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37755156/), [complete type-genome report, PMID 38651909](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38651909/).

GCF_003286595.3 (UMB3440; SAMN08895450) matches the independently defined mictus type identifier. LPSN also lists CP132985; its accession agrees with the cached first FASTA header NZ_CP132985.1. This is an accession-string bridge, not physical strain authentication. LPSN labels its sequence field as 16S rRNA; this review does not validate a sequence-feature interpretation. The DSMZ culture page has no CP132985 match, so the accession bridge is attributed specifically to LPSN.

GCF_003286735.3 (UMB1016; SAMN08895445) and GCF_008726475.3 (UMB7049; SAMN12797031) have no explicit type claims and were not matched to type aliases. UMB7049 must not be silently equated with the tenax type UMB3669. This says nothing about type genomes outside these three reviewed entries. Existing cross-label ANI is 95.15 and 95.05; their recorded 5th–95th percentile bounds straddle 95%. These bounds are retained as reported and are not reinterpreted as an independently calibrated confidence interval. No merger or relabeling is justified by this review.
